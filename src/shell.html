Checking this device…

Frames are rendered individually, not screen-recorded. Keep this tab visible and your phone unlocked. 4K is available on mobile when its encoder supports the selected settings; it is never silently downgraded.

The metronome is monitor-only. Original clip audio is muted. Very fast ramps and cuts are displayed on the nearest available video frame. SDR export; slow motion uses existing frames, not AI interpolation.

JUST ANIMATE EVERYTHING

A rhythm-first workflow.

Start with the music in your head.

Set BPM, choose the number of bars per clip, and add 1–20 videos. The default is 130 BPM, two 4-beat bars per clip, 24 fps, and 2160 × 3840 portrait output. “Make equal” gives every clip the same musical length.

Shape a moment, not just a transition.

Each clip starts at normal scale. The default Swoop rises from 1× to 2.5× speed in a 1/16 note, then eases to 0.55× in a 1/8 note while zooming to 1.65×. Ramp begins is measured in quarter-note beats from that clip’s start. Speed and zoom return in the final half-beat when enabled.

Changing BPM changes every time-based movement. Short slots compress motion phases if necessary. Fit-to-slot scales the entire speed curve to use the whole trimmed section; Loop and Hold keep your chosen speeds.

Arrange, preview, export.

Drag clips on desktop or use Move on mobile. Duplicate to repeat a moment. Space plays, the arrow keys step a frame, and Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes. Double-tap the left or right half of the preview to skip 5 seconds; focused-preview arrow keys do the same. Skipping keeps playback running until the end of the arrangement. The preview floats when you scroll below it, or press Pop out preview. Drag its title bar to move it, drag its corner to resize, and use ↗ to return. Alt-click the preview or use the framing sliders to adjust the zoom center. Metronome subdivisions go down to 1/32 notes and never appear in your export.

Export one clip, all clips separately, or the whole arrangement. Separate files use their matching soundtrack positions. Frame timestamps are calculated from the tempo; cuts are quantized to the output frame grid without accumulating per-clip timing drift.

Keep it local.

No video, project, or soundtrack leaves your browser. There is no Gemini integration, telemetry, account, server rendering, or watermark. The HTML embeds its CSS, JavaScript, and Mediabunny 1.57.0 runtime. The header and favicon use the adjacent logo.png file. Once loaded, editing and export do not need a network connection.

Know the edges.

Codec support and available memory depend on the device. Mobile 4K is not disabled, but the browser must pass an encoder check. Keep the app foregrounded while rendering. OPFS storage is used for exports when available; otherwise smaller renders use memory. Saving a portable project uses the original media bytes.

Clips’ original audio is intentionally muted; an optional soundtrack plays and exports at normal speed. Soundtracks are limited to five minutes / 80 MB to bound decoded-audio memory. This is an SDR renderer, not HDR grading, optical-flow slow motion, or an AI upscaler. Lower-resolution footage does not gain new detail when exported at 4K.
